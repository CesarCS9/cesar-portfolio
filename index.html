/// A little about me ///

I’ve always been fascinated by technology and computers. Combining that interest with my creative side is what led me to Front-End Development.

Taking an idea, putting time into the details, and turning it into something that actually works is one of the parts I enjoy most. I’m especially drawn to modern, minimal and thoughtful design, and adding those small interactions and details that make a website feel more alive.

Naturally patient and persistent, I like pushing myself to improve with every project. When something doesn’t work, I usually keep working at it until I find a way to solve it. Right now, my goal is to keep strengthening the skills I’ve built so far, gain more experience and eventually find my place in the industry.

Outside of coding, I’ve been playing guitar since I was a kid. Traveling, trying new food, gaming and keeping up with football are some of the things I enjoy in my free time.
